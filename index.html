C-Concept Studio × Vietnam 🧸

C-Concept is a London-based designer-toy studio founded in 2018, with manufacturing in Taiwan. Its hero character Unisaur is a 3-inch vinyl art toy with an interchangeable horn and clicking ratchet arms, released in colourful collections like Candy Garden, Sunday Morning and Midnight Botanical.

The brief: the brand was strong with 25–34-year-old collectors but struggled to reach younger fans. I helped plan the brand's communications and analyse the Vietnamese market so Unisaur could expand and reach new customers in Vietnam.

  1. 01
    Vietnam market analysisSized the toy market, tracked the 2024 art-toy boom (Labubu, Baby Three) and mapped e-commerce and retail channels: Shopee, TikTok Shop, Blindboxvn, MyKingdom and more.
  2. 02
    Consumer researchBilingual survey (English + Vietnamese) to define a new Vietnamese target segment and what makes them buy.
  3. 03
    Communication planningA 3-month social roadmap with objectives, formats, platforms, KPIs and content pillars for the Vietnam launch.
  4. 04
    Campaign conceptsInteractive Unisaur campaign ideas built on marketing-psychology levers.
